## Art Direction

KidWiz should feel bright, safe, modern, and magical without becoming scary or over-robotic. The generated image direction uses:

- white and soft sky-blue learning spaces
- lime-green primary energy
- teal, pink, purple, yellow, and light-blue subject accents
- soft 3D/2.5D educational illustration
- a gentle AI learning guide with a friendly face and warm safety cues
- no readable in-image text, fake logos, or watermarks

## Prompts

### Hero Learning World

Create a bright, modern, friendly hero illustration showing a warm AI learning guide helping two children explore school skills and life skills in a magical learning studio. Use a clean white and soft sky-blue learning space with floating subject worlds for science, reading, money, creativity, friendship, and confidence. Make it a high-quality 3D/2.5D premium educational brand image with soft rounded shapes, bright white surfaces, lime green primary accents, and teal, pink, purple, yellow, and light-blue subject accents. Use a wide hero composition with negative space around edges. Avoid readable text, logos, scary robots, dark cyberpunk styling, clutter, distorted faces or hands, and watermarks.

### Quest Map

Create a bright kid-friendly quest map illustration for a family learning app, showing a playful learning path across floating islands for science, reading, money, friendship, confidence, and digital safety. Use a clean white learning-world environment, soft sky-blue depth, floating path nodes, badges, stars, small flags, gentle clouds, and subtle grid-paper learning cues. Include a tiny friendly AI learning spark guide near the route. Use a premium 3D/2.5D educational illustration style with soft rounded forms and KidWiz color accents. Avoid readable text, logos, scary robots, weapons, dark fantasy, and clutter.

### Course Lab

Create a polished educational illustration showing a friendly lesson lab where academic learning, money habits, confidence, creativity, and family skills all appear as hands-on practice stations. Use bright white learning-studio surfaces, rounded worktables, a science experiment station, story cards, a piggy bank and coins, art supplies, a calm-reset heart badge, and a simple digital-safety shield. Include a small friendly AI spark guide. Use premium 3D/2.5D KidWiz styling and avoid readable text, logos, watermarks, dark screens, scary robots, clutter, and distorted hands or faces.

### Story And Journal

Create a warm visual for a children learning app about reflection, choices, friendship repair, confidence, and private journaling. Use a cozy bright reading nook inside a modern learning studio, with an open storybook, branching path cards, a heart-shaped confidence token, friendship cue, private journal notebook, mood stickers, and soft glowing stars. Include two children calmly choosing story cards with a small friendly AI spark guide nearby. Parent trust should be implied by a gentle shield or lock symbol near the journal. Avoid sad or scary mood, therapy/medical feel, surveillance cameras, readable text, logos, distorted faces or hands, and dark backgrounds.

### Learning Studio

Create a friendly AI tutor studio visual that feels safe for parents and exciting for children, showing a warm tutor guide helping explain an idea in multiple ways. Use a bright modern learning studio with white surfaces, soft blue light, visual explanation cards, quiz tokens, notebook cards, parent summary card shapes, and a safety shield motif. Center one gentle non-scary AI spark/robot guide with a glowing lime heart or leaf mark. Surround it with simple visual idea bubbles for science, reading, money, and confidence. Avoid readable text, logos, watermarks, dark neon, surveillance, complex code, and adult office dashboard styling.
